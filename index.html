How To Choose

Which Laptop Specs Matter Most?

Avoid paying for features you don't need. Here is the exact specification priority hierarchy for each major workload.

Gaming

1 Dedicated GPU ▶
2 Thermal Cooling ▶
3 Refresh Rate (144Hz+) ▶
4 CPU & RAM

The Graphics Card (GPU) is 80% of your FPS. Never sacrifice GPU quality for a high CPU in a gaming machine.

Coding & Dev

1 Multi-Core CPU ▶
2 RAM (16GB - 32GB) ▶
3 Tactile Keyboard ▶
4 Battery & NVMe SSD

Compilation and Docker containers consume CPU cores and RAM. High RAM prevents lag when running virtual machines.

Editing

1 CPU + GPU Acceleration ▶
2 RAM (32GB for 4K) ▶
3 Color Screen (DCI-P3) ▶
4 Fast NVMe Storage

Video rendering relies on hardware encoder acceleration. High color accuracy (sRGB/DCI-P3) ensures accurate color grading.

College

1 Battery Life (10+ Hrs) ▶
2 Portability (<3.5 lbs) ▶
3 CPU Efficiency ▶
4 RAM & Price

Students move between lectures all day. Focus on low weight and battery endurance so you don't need a wall outlet.
